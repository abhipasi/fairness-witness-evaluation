Development note: the batch=15000 point was originally attempted as the third sweep
point but was replaced with batch=8000 after two independent attempts both showed
reproducibly low finalization (~40%, driven by genuine, sustained peer-connection
instability during those specific runs -- confirmed via repeated transport_error/
peer_reader_exit events spanning the full campaign duration, not just end-of-run
cleanup). VDF timing itself remained clean and stable even in those degraded runs
(2648.5ms mean, 21.2ms stdev on the 12 rounds that did finalize), confirming VDF
computation is genuinely independent of consensus-layer network conditions -- but
batch=15000's unreliability (consistent with it sitting past the throughput
saturation "knee" identified in separate throughput-benchmarking work) made it a poor
choice for a clean, reproducible sweep point. batch=8000 was selected as a
known-reliable substitute.
